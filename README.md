# Trade Finance Research

> Exploring how verified, auditable supply chain data can enable innovative financial services for logistics operators, small carriers, and SMEs.

## 🎯 Research Focus

### Working Capital Solutions
- **Challenge:** Small transport companies face cash flow constraints during digitalization
- **Opportunity:** Verified trade data enables lenders to assess creditworthiness accurately
- **Goal:** Unlock micro-financing for digitalization investments

### Financial Inclusion for Logistics MSMEs
- Overcoming barriers to digital adoption
- Risk modeling using supply chain data
- Sustainable profitability during transition
- Bridging digitalization cost gaps

### Trade Document Verification
- Digital proof of transport operations
- Cryptographic integrity of shipping documents
- Immutable audit trails for lender confidence
- Cross-border verification

## 💰 Financial Service Models

### Micro-Financing Against Verified Trade Documents
- Loans secured by digitalized transport records
- Risk assessment using immutable operational data
- Automated underwriting frameworks
- Settlement and repayment tied to transaction verification

### Working Capital Optimization
- Accelerated cash conversion cycles
- Factoring with verified documentation
- Real-time liquidity management
- Compliance-linked incentives

## 📊 Risk Modeling

Key factors in assessing logistics operator creditworthiness:
- Route consistency and volume
- Payment history (verified on-chain)
- Regulatory compliance records
- Operational efficiency metrics

## 🔗 Ecosystem Partnerships

- Fintech providers
- Traditional trade finance institutions
- Logistics operators and associations
- Regulatory authorities
